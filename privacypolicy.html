Privacy Policy

Reino Oscuro Gaming
Last updated: 2026

Reino Oscuro Gaming operates a Discord bot designed to manage and provide functionality for the Reino Oscuro Gaming community.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and how it is protected.

1. Information We Collect

The bot may collect and process the following data:

  • Discord User IDs
  • Discord Usernames and discriminators
  • Discord Role IDs
  • Message content only when required for command execution
  • Interaction data (buttons, slash commands, command usage logs)

The bot does not collect:

  • Email addresses
  • IP addresses
  • Real names
  • Payment or financial information

2. How We Use Your Information

Collected data is used only for:

  • Executing bot commands
  • Managing server functionality
  • Logging administrative actions
  • Moderation and server management
  • Improving bot stability and performance

Data is never sold, shared, or transferred to third parties.

3. Data Storage

  • Logs are stored locally or on private servers controlled by Reino Oscuro Gaming.
  • Data is retained only as long as necessary for operational purposes.
  • No unnecessary long-term data storage is performed.

4. User Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request deletion of your logged data
  • Ask what data is stored related to your account
  • Stop using the bot at any time by leaving the Discord server

For any request, contact a server administrator.

5. Third-Party Services

The bot operates exclusively within Discord and complies with:

  • Discord Terms of Service
  • Discord Developer Policy

No external analytics or tracking services are used.

6. Security

Reasonable technical and administrative measures are in place to protect user data against unauthorized access or misuse.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary. Changes will be reflected with a revised date.

8. Contact

For questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the administrators of Reino Oscuro Gaming through the official Discord server.
